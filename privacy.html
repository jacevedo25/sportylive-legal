Privacy policy for the beta version

24 November 2026 · version 2026-11-24.2

This policy describes how SportyLive handles your data during the beta and the commitments in force while it lasts. It is published at https://legal.sportylive.app/privacy.

What SportyLive is and who is responsible

SportyLive is an app to prepare and stream games with an integrated scoreboard. The person responsible for SportyLive is Josué Acevedo Carrión.

For privacy questions, requests about your data, questions or complaints, write to support@sportylive.app.

SportyLive has no accounts of its own: you do not sign up or create a SportyLive password.

  • support@sportylive.app

Information the app uses

Games and customization: teams (name, abbreviation and color), sport, rules, score, archived games, settings and the logos you import. They are kept on this phone.

Camera and microphone: with your permission, for the preview, the microphone test and streaming picture and sound when you press «Start broadcast». The scoreboard and logos are added to the video sent.

Photos and files: only the file you choose with the system picker, to use it as a logo.

Manual destination: the server address and stream key you type, kept in this phone's Keychain.

Google and YouTube, if you connect YouTube: Google sign-in returns your name, email and account identifier. The app uses the YouTube API to read your channel (name, identifier and @handle) and to create, check, bind, end or delete the broadcasts and the stream that SportyLive creates. It also uses the title, visibility, made-for-kids answer and status of those broadcasts.

Remote scoring, if you use it: an anonymous identity is created in the Supabase service, with no email or name. The game header (sport, teams without logos and rules), scoring actions with their time, the name you type for each phone and the pairing between phones are sent. The server also records when each game was last used.

Technical information: the app keeps on this phone a diagnostics log of broadcasts (speed, temperature and battery) that is not sent, limited to about 1 MB and deleted after 7 days without use. External services process technical data such as the IP address. SportyLive includes no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools of its own.

What it is used for

To show the scoreboard, keep and recover your games and settings, stream to the destination you choose, connect your channel, create and manage broadcasts as you act, recover an interrupted broadcast and allow remote scoring.

Google and YouTube data are used only for those channel, stream and broadcast features. SportyLive does not sell data or use it for advertising or to track you across apps.

SportyLive uses the YouTube API Services. The permission Google shows is broad (see, edit and delete YouTube content); SportyLive does not read, edit or delete videos, comments or captions. It can only delete a broadcast created by the app itself that never streamed, after its safety checks.

Where it is kept and who receives it

On this phone: games, settings and logos. In the iOS Keychain: the manual destination key, the Google session (kept by Google's own component) and the YouTube broadcast records.

Google and YouTube receive the authorization and channel and broadcast management requests and, when you stream to YouTube, the picture, sound and graphic.

The manual destination provider receives the signal and the key when you choose it.

Supabase receives the remote scoring data described above and keeps it on servers in the United States (Ohio). Only the phones paired to that game see it. SportyLive's administrator only sees counts of pending deletions, never data about people or games.

During the beta, feedback and diagnostics you send with TestFlight are handled by Apple.

Viewers see the broadcast according to the chosen visibility. «Unlisted» is not «Private»: anyone with the link can watch it.

Retention and deletion

YouTube data on this phone (copy of the channel and broadcast records): deleted if YouTube does not confirm it for 30 days. The app deletes it when it opens, before showing or using it; while the app is closed it cannot delete it, but it is only on this phone and is not sent anywhere.

If Google answers that the authorization is no longer valid (for example, because you removed it from your Google account), the app deletes that channel's data on this phone at that moment.

«Disconnect YouTube» revokes the authorization at Google and deletes that channel's data on this phone. Before, it tells you about broadcasts that might still be open and offers to end them; if they cannot be ended, you will need to manage them in YouTube Studio.

«Remove from this phone only» deletes the Google session on the phone, but the authorization stays in your Google account until you remove it.

«Delete YouTube data from this phone» (Settings or Destination) deletes the Google session on the phone, the copy of the channel, visibility and titles, and every broadcast record and its pending tasks. It does not revoke the authorization at Google or delete videos or broadcasts on YouTube.

Archived games: you can delete one or all of them from «Archived games», with confirmation. Settings and archived games are also deleted by uninstalling the app. Logos are deleted when you remove them. Uninstalling does not guarantee that the iOS Keychain is cleared.

The microphone test is deleted when you close it. The diagnostics log is kept in the phone's cache, up to about 1 MB and 7 days; iOS may also clear it.

Remote scoring: «Delete my remote scoring data» (Settings) deletes from the server the games created from this phone and the anonymous identity. In other cameras' games, your plays are kept so their score does not change, but the phone's name is deleted and they are no longer linked to it; in plays created by earlier versions of the app, part of the identifier may remain until that game is deleted. Offline, the request is saved and sent when the connection is back. It does not delete the current game or the archived games on this phone, or what other phones already received.

Without the phone we cannot check which remote data is yours, so we cannot delete it on request by email. Until you delete it, remote data is kept on the server; it is planned to delete it automatically 30 days after the game's last activity, and this policy will be updated when that is turned on.

To be able to repeat deletions if the server had to be recovered, a list of the deleted anonymous identifiers is kept, with no game data. The server's current plan has no automatic backups: a serious failure could lose remote scoring data. Supabase also keeps technical logs (for example, IP addresses) under its own terms.

Deleting SportyLive data does not delete videos or other data on YouTube: manage them in YouTube or YouTube Studio.

Security

Keys and the Google session are kept in the iOS Keychain, and connections are encrypted (HTTPS and RTMPS; with a manual destination, encryption depends on the address you type). The anonymous remote scoring session is kept in the app's storage. No system is completely secure: protect your phone and accounts, and do not share keys in screenshots or feedback.

Minors and sports content

SportyLive is meant for adults responsible for the broadcast. Before streaming, make sure you have the permissions needed from the people shown, the venue and the logo owners.

Each new YouTube broadcast asks whether it is made for kids, with no answer preselected.

  • YouTube guide to made-for-kids content

Your choices and changes

You can manage camera, microphone and photo permissions in iPhone Settings, use the manual settings without connecting Google, disconnect YouTube, delete YouTube data from this phone, remove the authorization from your Google account, delete archived games and delete your remote scoring data.

When this policy changes its version is updated, and the app asks you to accept it again before using YouTube.
